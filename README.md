## About

I'm **Lê Hoàng Anh**, a Computer Science student with a focus on **Data Science** at the **[Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT)](https://english.ptit.edu.vn/)** in Vietnam.

My interests sit at the intersection of **artificial intelligence and backend engineering**. I want to understand both how models learn from data and how software systems make those models useful, especially in applications involving language and visual information.
